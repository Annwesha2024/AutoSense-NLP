# Dataset Information

## Synthetic Development Dataset

`raw/synthetic/automotive_reviews.csv` contains 30 synthetic automotive
reviews created specifically for development, testing, and demonstration
of AutoSense-NLP.

These reviews are not presented as real-world customer data.

## MuSe-CarASTE

AutoSense-NLP was also developed and evaluated using the MuSe-CarASTE
automotive aspect-sentiment dataset.

Repository:
https://github.com/AtiUsm/MuseASTE

The MuSe-CarASTE repository states that its work is licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International License
(CC BY-NC 4.0) for noncommercial academic and research purposes.

The raw MuSe-CarASTE files are intentionally excluded from this repository.
Users who have the appropriate access may place the following files in:

`data/raw/muse_caraste/`

Required files:

- `train.csv`
- `devel.csv`
- `FinalFullAnnotationsAllLabelsCompleted.csv`

The dataset should be cited as:

Usmani, A., Alsamhi, S. H., Khan, M. J., Breslin, J., & Curry, E.
"MuSe-CarASTE: A comprehensive dataset for aspect sentiment triplet
extraction in automotive review videos."
Expert Systems with Applications, 262, 125695, 2025.

DOI:
10.1016/j.eswa.2024.125695

Original MuSe-Car dataset/challenge should also be cited where applicable.
